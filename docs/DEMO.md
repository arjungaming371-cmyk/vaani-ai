# Demo Video Script — 5 minutes max (matches the official required contents)

## Required sections (from submission guidelines) mapped to timestamps

### 1. The problem we're solving (0:00–0:40)
Open on a phone with missed calls + unread WhatsApp pile. Say: "This is an average Tuesday for a small business in India. Every missed enquiry is a customer who went to a competitor. SMEs lose over half their leads not because their service is bad — but because nobody answered in time."

### 2. Why it matters (0:40–1:10)
"63 million MSMEs face this. Hiring a telecaller costs Rs 15-25K/month for 8 hours in one or two languages. And 90% of Indian customers prefer speaking their own language. The official problem statement says it directly: businesses lose potential customers because enquiries are not followed up efficiently."

### 3. Our solution — overview (1:10–1:50)
Show the dashboard. "Vaani is an AI front-desk. It answers every phone call and WhatsApp message instantly, in Telugu, Hindi or English, qualifies the lead, and follows up automatically. It's fully self-hosted — the AI runs on the business's own machine, so conversations cost nothing and customer data never leaves the server."

### 4. LIVE demonstration (1:50–4:00) — the core
a. (1:50) Dial the demo number ON CAMERA. Speak Telugu: ask for an appointment tomorrow.
b. (2:20) Vaani answers in Telugu, asks name and preferred time. Let the conversation breathe — this is the wow moment.
c. (2:50) Cut to dashboard: the lead appeared LIVE with transcript + extracted name/need.
d. (3:10) WhatsApp booking link arrives automatically on the phone. Tap, fill, submit.
e. (3:30) Dashboard status flips to "Booked". Show the ✓✓ delivery ticks.
f. (3:40) Script Manager: switch business type from clinic to coaching centre in one dropdown — "same agent, completely different business, zero code."

### 5. Uniqueness + impact (4:00–4:50)
"Three things make Vaani different: it's voice-first — a real phone conversation, not just a chatbot; it's self-hosted — zero per-conversation cost and total data privacy; and it's one product for any business — the owner edits the script, not the code. The impact: every enquiry answered in under 3 seconds, 24x7, in three languages, at one-twentieth the cost of a single telecaller."

### 6. Close (4:50–5:00)
"Vaani AI. Build today, impact tomorrow." → end card with GitHub + MVP links.

## Recording tips
- Screen-record dashboard at 1080p; film the phone separately; edit side by side for the live call segment.
- Keep the AI's Telugu answer audible and unedited — authenticity beats polish.
- Upload to YouTube as UNLISTED, then test the link in an incognito window before submitting.
